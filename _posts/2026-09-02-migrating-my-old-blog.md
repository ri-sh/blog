I started blogging back in 2014, mostly random posts about whatever I was messing with at the time — OpenCV, scraping football stats, some machine learning stuff, a corner detector implementation, that kind of thing. It sat on Blogspot for about ten years at [rishabhroy.blogspot.com](https://rishabhroy.blogspot.com/).

I've moved 27 old posts over here, images included, dates kept as they were — pulled from a full Blogger export rather than just what was publicly listed, so a few posts that never made it onto the live blog are here too. A handful of empty or unfinished drafts didn't make the cut. Nothing rewritten content-wise, just cleaned up and ported — so expect some rough edges and 2014-era code that probably doesn't run anymore.

Going forward, new posts land here instead.
