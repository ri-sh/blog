I started blogging back in 2014, mostly random posts about whatever I was messing with at the time — OpenCV, scraping football stats, some machine learning stuff, a corner detector implementation, that kind of thing. It sat on Blogspot for about ten years at [rishabhroy.blogspot.com](https://rishabhroy.blogspot.com/).

I've moved all 21 old posts over here, images included, dates kept as they were. Nothing rewritten, just ported as-is — so expect some rough edges and 2014-era code that probably doesn't run anymore.

Going forward, new posts land here instead.
